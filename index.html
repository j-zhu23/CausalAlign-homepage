Method

CausalAlign identifies where a video generation model begins to forget ego-motion, then aligns those internal states with motion-centric delta tokens from a 3D foundation model.

01

Build the teacher delta-token space

Encode changes between frames with a 3D foundation model, separating ego-motion from static scene content.

02

Train the VGM projector

Map VGM feature transitions into the teacher delta-token space, then freeze the projector.

03

Fine-tune selected VGM layers

Apply delta-token supervision through LoRA adapters at the layers identified by causal diagnosis.

Quantitative Results

The diagnosis tracks internal ego-motion across denoising. Camera-motion benchmarks then measure geometry and temporal consistency, while visual-quality scores check that the gains do not come from degraded appearance.
